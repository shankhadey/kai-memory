# Research and benchmarks

## What happened

**Organizational-reasoning benchmark (Feb–Oct 2026)**
- This went relatively well. It had a clear goal and structured commits: scoring pipeline, contamination controls, Core/Test vs Challenge splits.
- Late reshuffles show the plan shifting midway: the splits were rebalanced 3 times in two days ("Plan B"), and the answer keys moved to a private repo after the scenarios had been created.
- One session spent a while comparing its plan with a branch it hadn't read first.

## Rules

1. **Decide the protocol before generating data:**
   - splits and their sizes
   - contamination and answer-key policy (private from day 1)
   - judge model and rubric
   - target sample sizes
2. **Before planning, read what already exists:** branches, papers, the collaborator's repo.
3. **Run a cheap pilot** (10 items, 2 models) before scaling. Check that the judge agrees with a human on a sample.
