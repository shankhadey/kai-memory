# Launch and marketing

## What happened

**TV remote launch (Nov 2026)**
- Late changes that rippled through the code: selling it, the country list, Portuguese, the Play Store, the name and package ID (still pending).
- The demo video took about 6 rounds:
  - no cause-and-effect script
  - placeholder screens
  - what was tapped didn't match what opened
  - screens too short to read

## Rules

1. **Week 1, not launch week:**
   - name and trademark check
   - domain
   - package ID
   - store account type (a company account skips the 14-day test with 12 testers)
   - privacy policy URL
2. **Store screenshots stay generic.** No third-party logos or posters.
3. **Demo videos:**
   - Write a cause-and-effect script: what's tapped → what appears.
   - Use real screens.
   - Phone and screen always agree.
   - Each shot holds at least 1 s longer than feels necessary.
   - Kai watches it as a stranger before sending.
   - Check network access and keys before starting.
4. **Distribution plan alongside the build,** not after: who shares it, which communities, which keywords.
